Freecash review: is it really worth your time?

Freecash is a reward platform that pays for small tasks: trying apps, playing games up to a certain level and answering surveys. This review sticks to what the platform publicly says and the drawbacks you should know about. It is not a full-time income.

Short verdict: A possible small side income if you read each offer carefully and know your hourly rate. If you expect quick money, you will be disappointed.
Where it is available: Freecash's referral programme only pays when the new user lives in the UK, Canada, Australia, Switzerland, South Korea or Japan. I use a Swiss account and tell you this openly.

Payouts

According to Freecash's help centre you can cash out via PayPal, bank transfer, gift cards and crypto. The minimum is roughly 5 to 20 dollars depending on region and method. For your first payout you must verify your email and your ID (selfie plus document).

Pros and cons

Pros: free to join, small tasks for spare minutes, several payout options.
Cons: high rewards usually need a lot of play time or in-game spending. One account per person, no VPN. Surveys pay little and ask for personal data. ID check is mandatory.
